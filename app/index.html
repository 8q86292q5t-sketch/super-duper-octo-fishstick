    Recording tips for a good voice clone
    • Use the same quiet room, same mic and same distance (about a hand's width) for every session.
    • Wired earbuds with a mic or a headset mic beat the phone's built-in mic.
    • Speak naturally, in the tone you want the clone to have. Read punctuation: questions should sound like questions.
    • Record in several short sessions instead of one marathon; stop when your voice gets tired.
    • Say exactly what is written. If you stumble, retry the take.
    • Aim for every phrase. More clean audio = a better voice. ~30 min of speech is a solid start.

      How do I get “my voice model”?

      The phone collects the data; a computer trains the voice once. Sync or export your recordings, train a Piper voice from them (see README), then run server.py --model your-voice.onnx. This page will then speak any text in your voice, fully offline on your own network.

      My recordings mode works right away: it plays back your takes for phrases or single words you have recorded.
